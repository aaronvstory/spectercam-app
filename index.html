Compared

SpecterCam vs New Camera vs CHMP4.

All three put a video into the camera, from a file or an OBS / RTMP feed. What sets them apart is latency, whether you can see the feed, and whether you drive it from a computer. Facts here are from our own teardown of the shipping apps.

From our own teardown of the shipping apps. All three take an OBS / RTMP feed. SpecterCam bundles the server, so there is nothing to run, and adds a low‑latency USB link. An RTMP feed is where the lag comes from. New Camera is free and simple. CHMP4 is a capable paid app with deep camera-stack hooking. One shared ceiling: no frame-replay tool, SpecterCam included, beats a real-time liveness challenge.

Part of a set

The camera is one piece.

SpecterCam handles the camera feed. Specter handles the rest of the phone: its model, its identifiers, its whole device story. They are built to run together on the same device.

SpecterCam: the camera

Puts your video where the lens should be. Feed it however you like, see it live, and every camera app reads it.

Specter: the device

Gives the phone a fresh, consistent device identity per account: model, build, identifiers, radio and more. The camera and the device, covered by two tools that fit together.
